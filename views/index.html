Frequently Asked Questions

Is the converter free to use?
Yes! CrevoDoc is completely free to use. You can convert documents, slides, and images without any restrictions or watermarks.
Are my files stored securely on the server?
Absolutely. We value your privacy. Uploaded files are converted in temporary storage and are permanently deleted after 30 minutes. We do not expose files through public URLs.
What file sizes are supported?
We support uploading files up to 50MB for Word, PowerPoint, and PDF files, and up to 20MB for individual image conversions.
Can I convert multiple images at once?
Yes, our image converters (JPG to PDF and PNG to PDF) allow you to upload multiple images at the same time, rotate them, and drag-and-drop to reorder them before merging them into a single PDF.
Is PDF to Word conversion editable?
Yes! Our converter tries to reconstruct the layout, paragraphs, tables, and images of your PDF into an editable Word document (.docx) rather than rendering them as flat images.
Can I use the converter on mobile devices?
Yes, CrevoDoc is fully responsive and optimized to run perfectly on desktop computers, laptops, tablets, and smartphones.
